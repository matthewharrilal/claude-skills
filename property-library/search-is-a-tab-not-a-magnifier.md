# Search is a tab, not a magnifier

If search is central to discovery, it gets a tab. Hiding it behind a nav-bar icon signals it's an escape hatch, not a path.

## What it means

Search as a tab is an explicit invitation to use the app even when you don't know its taxonomy. Search as a magnifier icon in the nav bar is a fallback — a thing you reach for when the visible navigation fails you. The two placements carry opposite messages about the app's confidence in search. Apps with rich discovery surfaces promote search to tab status because the act of typing is a primary way users engage with the catalog.

## Exemplars

- **Apple Music** — Search is one of the five pillars
- **Spotify** — Search is a top-level pillar of discovery
- **Instagram** — Search/Explore is a dedicated tab
- **App Store** — Search has its own pillar slot

## Violators (apps that pay the price)

- **Notion iOS** — pre-redesigns hid search behind a magnifier
- **Bear** — search lives behind a glass icon, signaling "for power users"

## Why it matters at AAA quality

Search-as-tab signals "you're allowed to not know the taxonomy." Search-as-icon assumes you know what to type. The tab placement is a generosity to new and returning users alike.
