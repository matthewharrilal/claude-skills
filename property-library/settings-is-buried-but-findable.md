# Settings is buried but findable

Settings lives in a consistent, unsurprising place — usually the profile tab or a gear in the top-right of the home tab. Never on the tab bar itself.

## What it means

Settings is a rarely-visited surface — the user opens it a handful of times across the lifetime of the install. Promoting it to a tab wastes one of the four or five pillars of the IA on something that doesn't deserve that real estate. AAA apps place settings somewhere conventional (profile tab, gear icon in the nav bar) so it's findable when needed without consuming a pillar slot.

## Exemplars

- **Apple's first-party apps** — settings live inside the app or in system Settings.app; never as a tab
- **Instagram** — settings under the profile tab
- **Apple Music** — settings nested under profile / account

## Violators (apps that pay the price)

- **Apps that tab-promote "Settings"** — waste a pillar slot on a rarely-used surface

## Why it matters at AAA quality

Tab bar real estate is sacred. Settings doesn't deserve it. The tabs should each represent a primary mode of use, and configuration is not a primary mode.
