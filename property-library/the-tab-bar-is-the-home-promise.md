# The tab bar is the home promise

The root tab bar represents the app's whole world. From any depth, the user can return to any pillar with a single tap.

## What it means

Tabs are not a navigation widget — they are a contract about what the app is. Each tab is a non-negotiable territory of the product, not a feature-of-the-month that may be rearranged in the next release. The promise is that the user's spatial model of "where things live" survives across sessions, releases, and depth of navigation.

## Exemplars

- **Apple Music** — Listen Now, Browse, Radio, Library, Search; the five tabs map to the entire music-listening world
- **Instagram** — the five-tab world has remained semantically stable for years
- **Cash App** — each pillar is a complete sub-product, not a feature drawer

## Violators (apps that pay the price)

- **Notion iOS** — root is a workspace tree masquerading as navigation; there is no stable pillar set
- **Airbnb** — has churned tab semantics across redesigns, breaking returning users' spatial models

## Why it matters at AAA quality

Without a stable root, users build no spatial model. Every session starts disoriented. The tab bar is the only persistent landmark in a deep app; when it shifts meaning, the whole product feels untrustworthy.
