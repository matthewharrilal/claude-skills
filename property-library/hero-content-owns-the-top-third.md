# Hero content owns the top third

The most important thing on the screen lives in the top third at full visual weight. Everything else is supporting cast.

## What it means

Visual hierarchy must answer "what is this screen about?" instantly, before the user reads any text. The top third is the highest-attention real estate because it's both visually first and the entry point for scroll. AAA apps reserve it for the hero — the artwork, the card, the image, the title — and trim the chrome above and around it ruthlessly. Cluttered nav bars and toolbars that compress the hero into a middle band rob the screen of its identity.

## Exemplars

- **Apple Music** — the now-playing card dominates the top of the player surface
- **Apple Wallet** — the card itself is the hero, with chrome demoted to the edges
- **Photos** — the image is the screen; chrome dissolves to give the photo full weight

## Violators (apps that pay the price)

- **Apps with cluttered nav bars and toolbars** — compress the hero into a middle band, removing its dominance

## Why it matters at AAA quality

Without a clear hero, the screen has no answer to "what is this?" — the user processes chrome instead of content. The hero IS the screen's identity.
