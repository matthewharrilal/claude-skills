# Negative space as confidence

Generous padding signals "I know what's important." Cramped layouts signal "I had to fit it all." Double the whitespace when uncertain.

## What it means

Whitespace is not absence — it is presence. It says: I selected this content because it matters, and I'm willing to give up screen real estate to let it stand. Cramped layouts read as anxious; spacious ones read as confident. When in doubt, double the padding.

## Exemplars

- **Apple Wallet** — generous breathing room around each card
- **Fantastical** — event detail spacious
- **Linear** — hero-level whitespace throughout
- **Cash App home** — confidence through emptiness

## Mechanisms in UIKit/SwiftUI

- Liberal use of Spacer()
- Large contentInset / layoutMargins

## What goes wrong without it

UI feels desperate, untrustworthy. Reduces perceived quality more than any other factor — more than typography choices, more than color, more than animation.
