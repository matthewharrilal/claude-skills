## Regulatory Trajectory Indicators

### "Gradual Tightening" Patterns (Historical Parallels)

| Phase | Historical Equivalent | Crypto Manifestation | Status |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------|--------|
| Rhetoric shift | "Currency speculators," "hoarders" | "Crypto is for criminals," "investor protection" | Ongoing |
| Information gathering | Census, registration requirements | Exchange reporting requirements, travel rule | Implemented |
| Voluntary → mandatory | "Encouraged" compliance becoming required | KYC expansion, DeFi frontend geoblocking | Accelerating |
| Threshold tightening | Reich Flight Tax threshold drops | Reporting thresholds lowering, presumptive violations | In progress |
| Exit restrictions | Capital controls, exit taxes | Withdrawal limits, forced liquidations, unrealized gains taxation proposals | Proposed/partial |
| Full capture | Asset seizure, forced conversion | Stablecoin bans, self-custody restrictions, CBDC mandates | Discussed |

## "Act Now vs. Act Later" Decision Framework

### Do Now (Preparation Phase)

Actions to take while optionality exists:

- Establish self-custody with tested recovery
- Build P2P trading relationships/reputation
- Set up accounts in multiple jurisdictions
- Run your own node (or have ability to)
- Diversify across chains and asset types
- Understand direct contract interaction
- Maintain operational security hygiene
- Document your own holdings (encrypted)

### Act When (Warning Signs)

Triggers that should accelerate preparation:

- Your jurisdiction proposes self-custody restrictions
- Exchanges in your region face coordinated banking termination
- Privacy coin delistings accelerate
- Travel rule implementation tightens
- Political rhetoric specifically targets crypto holders
- Capital controls implemented on traditional finance
- Stablecoin issuer faces existential regulatory action

### Too Late After

Points of no return (historical patterns):

- Bank holiday declared / exchange withdrawals frozen
- Retroactive legislation criminalizing prior legal activity
- Asset registry requirements with short compliance window
- Emergency decree powers invoked
- Forced conversion at unfavorable rates already executed

## Jurisdictional Assessment Framework

When analyzing sources, assess jurisdictional trajectory:

| Factor | Positive Indicators | Negative Indicators |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Legal clarity | Clear frameworks, proportionate rules | Regulation by enforcement, vague statutes |
| Property rights | Strong tradition, independent judiciary | History of confiscation, political judiciary |
| Banking access | Crypto businesses can bank | Debanking campaigns |
| Self-custody stance | Explicitly legal | Restricted or proposed restrictions |
| Privacy stance | Legitimate privacy tools tolerated | Criminalization of privacy tech |
| Exit freedom | No capital controls | Exit taxes, capital controls |
| Political stability | Stable institutions | Authoritarian drift, emergency powers |
