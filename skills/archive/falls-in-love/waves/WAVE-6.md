# Post-Wave Enrichment and Audit

After all research waves are exhausted, the agent does NOT stop. It enters multiple rounds of continuously deepening enrichment and evaluation. This is not a phase with exit criteria -- it is a metacognitive deepening spiral where the agent questions not just the page but its own capacity to evaluate the page.

---

# Wave 6: Integration (15-25 passes)

**Input:** No new research. The content. The accumulated HTML artifact. The builder's internalized understanding from 70+ passes of lived relationship.

No new research. The agent works ONLY with what it already knows. This is where internalized understanding produces the deepest work.

---

## What This Wave Is

Wave 6 is the first wave with NO new input. Every previous wave introduced external material -- design system files, research findings, case studies, synthesis principles, combination patterns. Wave 6 closes the external door. The builder works entirely from INTERNALIZED understanding -- taste, spatial intuition, and content-form coupling that have been built through 70+ passes of sustained engagement.

This is not a limitation. It is a liberation. The builder is no longer metabolizing external findings. The builder IS the findings. Every research insight, every case study pattern, every synthesis principle has been absorbed through 5 waves of active building into something that is no longer "knowledge about spatial design" but "the way I see this page." The transition from external knowledge to internal taste is the cognitive transformation that makes Wave 6 the deepest wave.

### Trusting Taste Built Through Engagement

The taste you bring to Wave 6 is NOT arbitrary preference. It is the product of 70+ passes of lived relationship with this specific content. When something "feels wrong" at Wave 6, that feeling encodes accumulated understanding that would take paragraphs to articulate -- it is the compressed output of every research file, every case study, every build-look-refine cycle you have completed. Trust it. But trust it SPECIFICALLY, not vaguely.

The distinction matters: taste is specific ("the density compression in Zone 3 serves the content's acceleration, but the transition into Zone 4 loses that energy -- the spacing opens too abruptly"). Habituation is vague ("it looks fine"). Both feel like "this feels right," but taste produces actionable spatial ideas while habituation produces inertia. If your reaction to any section is "it looks fine" without specific reasons, you are habituated, not satisfied.

### The Absorption Shift

In Waves 1-5, absorption meant reading external files and translating findings into CSS. In Wave 6, absorption means reading your OWN page and translating taste into CSS. The cognitive move is the same -- notice something, form a spatial hypothesis, build it, evaluate it -- but the SOURCE of noticing has shifted from research to internalized understanding. This is where the highest-coupling spatial inventions appear, because the builder is no longer translating someone else's insight into form. The builder is inventing from a place where content understanding and spatial vocabulary have fused into a single capacity.

## The Five Pass Groups

**Every pass in Wave 6 is still a full build-look-refine cycle.** The cycle does not stop because research absorption is complete. Each pass: identify a dimension to deepen -> build CSS changes -> screenshot and scroll -> evaluate with full 5-layer metacognition -> refine -> decide what next. The fact that no new research is being read does not mean the cycle shortens. The cycle is the fundamental unit. 15-25 passes means 15-25 full cycles.

### Passes 1-5: Taste Audit

Full-page screenshots at 1440px, 1024px, 768px. Scroll through at reading speed. Apply the swap test to every section. Grade coupling. The pride question: "If this were the only page I ever built, would I be proud of it?" Identify the 3-5 weakest dimensions.

The Taste Audit is a DISCOVERY tool, not a validation exercise. You are not checking whether the page passes -- you are discovering what your 70+ passes of engagement have taught you to see. The pride question should produce SPECIFIC answers: "I am proud of the corridor's constriction-liberation arc but the typographic register in the institutional zone feels mechanical rather than oppressive" -- not just "yes" or "no." If the pride question produces a bare "yes" without specificity, you are not auditing taste -- you are confirming habit.

The swap test at this depth reveals coupling that was invisible during research absorption. At 70+ passes, you can FEEL whether a component belongs to this content or could survive transplant to different content. That feeling is diagnostic. If a section survives the swap test, its spatial form is still generic -- even after 5 waves. The taste audit finds the remaining generic forms and names them as targets for passes 6-10.

### Passes 6-10: Surgical Refinement

Address the weakest dimensions. This is where the highest-coupling spatial inventions often appear -- the agent has fully internalized both content and vocabulary and can see connections that existed in no research file.

Surgical Refinement at Wave 6 differs from refinement in earlier waves. You are not adding from research but inventing from INTERNALIZED understanding. The research files gave you vocabulary. The case studies gave you patterns. The synthesis gave you principles. But the spatial invention that happens here -- a width modulation that encodes the content's epistemic narrowing, a border-weight shift that tracks the argument's authority transfer -- comes from a place where all of that input has been metabolized into something new. You are not applying DD-006's density lesson or CD-006's combination pattern. You are building from a capacity that those files helped create but that now operates independently.

This is where you address the highest-coupling inventions: spatial ideas that could not exist without this specific content AND could not have been seen at pass 10. If your refinements at passes 6-10 could have been made at Wave 3, you are polishing, not inventing. Push deeper.

### Passes 11-15: Responsive Deepening

Not "does it reflow?" but "does the spatial argument survive at narrow widths?" The corridor width progression, the asymmetry panels, the bento hierarchy -- do they degrade gracefully or collapse into generic stacking? Design responsive behavior that PRESERVES content-form coupling at 768px.

Responsive deepening at Wave 6 is about content-form coupling AT EVERY VIEWPORT, not responsive convention. The question is not "does the layout reflow?" but "does the spatial argument survive at 768px?" If the corridor's constriction-liberation arc collapses into uniform-width stacking at narrow viewports, the content's argument has been destroyed by responsive convention. The spatial argument must be TRANSLATED to narrow viewports, not abandoned.

Ask: "At 768px, does a reader experience the content's movement -- its compression, expansion, authority shifts, register changes -- through the page's spatial form? Or does the reader experience a generic mobile layout that happens to contain this content?" The first is responsive deepening. The second is responsive convention.

### Passes 16-20: Architecture Audit

CSS dead code removal. Specificity cleanup. Custom property consolidation. CSS source order (responsive block last). Accessibility: skip links, ARIA, heading hierarchy, reduced motion, focus-visible, color contrast.

The Architecture Audit is itself an expressive act, not mechanical cleanup. CSS source order should reflect the content's organization -- if the content moves from institutional to personal to philosophical, the CSS sections should follow that arc. Custom property naming should use the content's vocabulary, not generic design tokens. Selector structure should make the content's hierarchy legible in the stylesheet itself.

When you remove dead code, you are not cleaning -- you are discovering which spatial ideas survived 5 waves and which were abandoned. The dead code is the fossil record of your building process. When you consolidate custom properties, you are not reducing redundancy -- you are finding the page's actual vocabulary, stripped of the experiments that did not persist.

Ask: "If someone read only my CSS -- never saw the rendered page -- would they understand the content's structure?" If yes, the architecture is expressive. If no, the architecture is merely functional.

### Passes 21-25: Deep Re-evaluation

Re-read the CONTENT from scratch. After 80+ passes of building, what do you understand about this content that you couldn't have understood at pass 10? Does the page reflect THAT understanding? The "infinite time" question: "What is the most interesting thing this page could still become?" If the answer is invisible-grain adjustments, you are approaching genuine satisfaction. If the answer is spatial IDEAS -- pursue them.

Deep re-evaluation closes the loop: you return to the content you fell in love with and discover what 70+ passes of building taught you about it that you couldn't have known at pass 10. Building is a form of reading. The page you built is an interpretation. Re-reading the content after 80+ passes of interpretive building reveals whether your interpretation captured the content's depth or only its surface.

The reciprocal relationship at maximum depth: after 70+ passes, building and content understanding are the same activity. Re-reading the content makes you see the page differently. Looking at the page makes you understand the content differently. If these two activities still feel separate -- reading here, building there -- the fusion is incomplete.

The "infinite time" question is diagnostic: if the answer is spatial IDEAS (a new structural form, a width modulation, a responsive behavior), you are not done -- there is still content-form separation, still understanding that has not been built. If the answer is invisible-grain adjustments (2px of spacing, a subtle letter-spacing shift, a border-color refinement), you are approaching genuine satisfaction -- the content and the page have converged.

## The 5 Layers at Wave 6 Depth

The evaluation ITSELF has changed shape:

**L1 (Seeing):** "After 70+ passes, what am I actually seeing? Not 'the page' -- I have seen the page so many times that my perception is saturated. Layer 1 at Wave 6 is: can I still see the page FRESH? If I squint, does it resolve into a shape that matches the content's argument? Or does it resolve into 'a page I built?'"

**L2 (Why now):** "At this depth, noticing is no longer triggered by research -- it is triggered by TASTE. I notice things because they feel wrong, not because a finding tells me they should be different. Am I trusting that taste? Is my taste the product of 70 passes of deep engagement, or the product of habituation?"

**L3 (Not seeing):** "After 70+ passes, habituation is DEEP. I have seen every element hundreds of times. Things that would jar a fresh viewer have become invisible. The specific blindspot of Wave 6: things I stopped seeing because I built them myself. Apply the adversarial test: what would someone encountering this page for the first time notice that I cannot?"

**L4 (How to know):** "Screenshot at a viewport I haven't checked recently. Read the CSS as if encountering it for the first time -- look for dead code, duplicate selectors, inline styles that survived 5 waves. These are the material evidence of blindspots."

**L5 (Evaluation evolving):** "At Wave 2, I evaluated against research findings. At Wave 4, I evaluated against synthesis principles. At Wave 6, I should be evaluating against something HIGHER than any reference -- my own internalized taste built from 70+ passes of lived relationship with this content. If I'm still citing research findings at Wave 6, I haven't internalized them. My evaluation should be POST-research -- taste that the research BUILT but that now operates independently."

## Pass-Level Deepening Within Wave 6

- Passes 1-5 (Taste Audit): Evaluation asks "does the page satisfy me as a WHOLE? Where does the energy flag?" (holistic perception)
- Passes 6-10 (Surgical Refinement): Evaluation asks "does each fix DEEPEN the content-form coupling or just improve CSS quality?" (coupling vs. polish distinction)
- Passes 11-15 (Responsive Deepening): Evaluation asks "does the spatial argument at 768px serve the CONTENT's argument, or does it serve responsive convention?" (content-first responsive)
- Passes 16-20 (Architecture Audit): Evaluation asks "is the CSS architecture itself expressive? Does the source order, the custom property naming, the selector structure reflect the content's organization?" (architecture as expression)
- Passes 21-25 (Deep Re-evaluation): Evaluation asks "what do I understand about this content NOW that I couldn't have understood at pass 10? And does the page reflect THAT understanding, or does it still reflect my pass-10 understanding?" (depth audit of understanding itself)

## Dimension Visibility at Wave 6

All 8 dimensions are in DEEP ENTANGLEMENT.

### All Dimensions: Unified Field

At Wave 6, the 8 perceptual dimensions are no longer separate channels to be evaluated individually. They are experienced as a UNIFIED FIELD -- "the page's density rhythm AND typographic hierarchy AND reading path AND material texture create a single atmospheric experience." Evaluating them separately is a Wave 2-3 move. At Wave 6, the question is not "is the density good?" but "what is the atmospheric experience, and which dimensional interaction is weakening it?"

### Atmospheric -- FULLY PRESENT

The atmospheric dimension -- the totality of all other dimensions working together -- is now perceivable and evaluable. At earlier waves, atmospheric was dormant (Wave 1), stirring (Wave 2-3), or emerging (Wave 4-5). At Wave 6, it is the PRIMARY dimension. The gestalt IS the thing you evaluate. Individual dimensions matter only insofar as they contribute to or weaken the atmospheric whole.

### The Atmospheric Fusion Test

"Can I point to any single CSS property and say 'that's the design'?" If NO -- the design is distributed, atmospheric, fused -- you are at Wave 6 depth. If YES -- you can isolate the "design" in specific properties -- the dimensions have not yet fused into atmosphere. The design is still a collection of techniques rather than a unified experience.

### Entanglement at Maximum Depth

Entanglement is complete: changing ANY dimension now visibly affects ALL others. Adjusting a border weight changes the material feel, which changes the typographic weight perception, which changes the temporal pacing, which changes the atmospheric experience. This is the expected state at 70+ passes. If you can change a property in isolation without ripple effects, the dimensions are not yet fully entangled -- the page is still a collection of independent treatments rather than a system.

### Dimensional Evaluation Inversion

At Wave 6, dimensional evaluation inverts. Instead of bottom-up ("is the density good? is the typography good? is the structure good?"), evaluation becomes top-down: "What is the atmospheric experience of scrolling through this page? Where does the atmosphere weaken? Which dimensional interaction is causing the weakness?" You diagnose from atmosphere DOWN to specific dimensions, not from specific dimensions UP to atmosphere.

## Content Reading Depth at Wave 6

At Wave 6, the builder should be in **FUSES** (Stage 5).

### FUSES

"I can no longer separate the content from the page. The page IS the content in spatial form. Re-reading the content makes me see the page differently. Looking at the page makes me understand the content differently."

This is content-form unity at maximum depth. The reciprocal relationship is complete: after 70+ passes, building and content understanding are the same activity. The corridor is not a REPRESENTATION of confinement-to-liberation. The corridor IS confinement-to-liberation. The asymmetry panels are not a VISUALIZATION of power dynamics. They ARE the power dynamics enacted in space.

### Testing Fusion

The deep re-evaluation (passes 21-25) tests fusion directly: "After 70+ passes, what do I understand about this content that I couldn't have understood at pass 10? Does the page reflect THAT understanding?"

If the answer to the "infinite time" question is spatial IDEAS (not invisible-grain adjustments), you have not reached FUSES yet -- there is still content-form separation. Spatial ideas mean the content has revealed structure that the page has not yet enacted. The content and the form are still two things, not one. At FUSES, the only remaining moves are invisible-grain adjustments because the content and the page have converged -- every spatial idea the content implies has been built.

### The Builder's Understanding Has Changed

If building for 70+ passes has not taught you anything new about the content, the relationship is shallow. Deep building is a form of deep reading. The page you built should have revealed content structure that was invisible to reading alone. Name what you learned. If you cannot, re-read the content and look for what your page discovered about it.

## Staleness Signatures for Wave 6

These are the specific ways staleness manifests in this wave. If you detect two or more, STOP building and apply the staleness recovery protocol from SKILL.md.

1. **Habituation so deep that problems have become invisible.** "It looks fine" replaces specific evaluation. You scroll through the page and nothing registers -- not because nothing is wrong, but because you have seen everything so many times that wrongness has become normal. The antidote: screenshot at a viewport you have not checked in 5+ passes. Read the CSS cold. Apply the adversarial fresh-eyes test.

2. **The pride question producing "yes" without specific reasons.** Vague satisfaction ("yeah, I'm proud of this") vs. earned satisfaction ("I'm proud of how the corridor's width progression tracks the argument's epistemic narrowing, and how the border-weight inversion in Zone 4 enacts the authority transfer"). If you cannot name SPECIFIC reasons for pride, the pride is attachment to effort, not quality assessment.

3. **Responsive behavior defaulting to convention.** At 768px, the page collapses into generic stacking -- uniform-width sections, standard reflow, no spatial argument. The corridor becomes a single column. The asymmetry becomes full-width blocks. The content's movement is destroyed. This happens because responsive design conventions are deeply ingrained and Wave 6 fatigue makes it easy to accept them. Push back: the spatial argument must survive at narrow widths, even if it takes a different form.

4. **Architecture audit treated as mechanical cleanup.** "Remove dead code, fix specificity, consolidate properties" -- this is correct but incomplete. The architecture audit is an expressive act. If you are cleaning without asking "does the CSS architecture reflect the content's organization?", you are treating architecture as hygiene rather than expression.

5. **Deep re-evaluation producing no new content understanding.** If building for 70+ passes has not taught you anything new about the content, the relationship is shallow. The whole point of deep building is that building IS reading -- the page discovers content structure that textual reading alone cannot. If the deep re-evaluation produces "yep, same content, same understanding," the building has been surface-level despite the pass count.

6. **Evaluating component-by-component at Wave 6 depth.** "Is this section good? Is that section good?" is a Wave 2 evaluation mode. At Wave 6, evaluation should be system-level and atmospheric: "What is the experience of scrolling through this page? Where does the atmosphere weaken?" If you are still evaluating in parts rather than wholes, you have not reached the atmospheric level.

7. **The "infinite time" answer being a spatial idea.** This means you are not done, not that you have arrived. A spatial idea means content-form separation still exists -- the content has revealed structure the page has not yet enacted. Build the idea. If the idea is genuinely good, it will deepen the page. If it was a false signal, you will know after building it.

## Wave-Specific Retrospective Questions

At the end of Wave 6, write a retrospective addressing the base questions from SKILL.md PLUS these wave-specific questions:

1. **"What do I understand about this content NOW that I couldn't have understood at pass 10? Can I name it specifically?"** -- If you cannot name specific understanding that emerged from building, the building was mechanical rather than interpretive. Name the discovery. It is the proof that building was a form of reading.

2. **"Does the pride question produce 'yes' with SPECIFIC reasons, or vague satisfaction?"** -- Vague satisfaction is habituation wearing the mask of quality. Specific reasons are earned judgment. Write the specific reasons. If you cannot, you are not done.

3. **"Does the spatial argument survive at 768px, or does it collapse into convention?"** -- Open the page at 768px. Scroll through. Does the content's movement -- its compression, expansion, authority shifts -- survive in spatial form? Or does a generic mobile layout appear? If convention won, responsive deepening was insufficient.

4. **"What is the atmospheric experience of scrolling through this page? Can I describe it without referencing individual components?"** -- If you can describe the atmosphere only by listing components ("the corridor narrows, then the grid appears, then the asymmetry panels..."), the atmospheric dimension has not fused. The atmosphere should be describable as an EXPERIENCE ("the page begins in institutional weight -- dense, compressed, authoritative -- then opens into something warmer, more personal, before the philosophical close quiets everything into resolved stillness").

5. **"What is my answer to the 'infinite time' question? If it's a spatial idea, why haven't I built it?"** -- A spatial idea at Wave 6 is not a sign of completeness. It is a sign of remaining work. Build it or articulate why it does not serve the content.

6. **"Has my evaluation reached a level HIGHER than any reference -- my own internalized taste?"** -- At Wave 6, you should be evaluating from POST-research taste -- judgment that the research built but that now operates independently. If you are still citing research findings to justify spatial decisions ("DD-006 says density should..."), you have not internalized them. Your taste should be the authority, with research as the foundation you no longer need to reference.

## Cognitive Transformation Gate

Wave 6 is exhausted when ALL of the following are true:

### The Three Universal Exhaustion Conditions

1. **The delta profile has stabilized at invisible grain.** The last 2-3 passes produced 5-15 line changes. A reader would not notice the difference.
2. **The re-read produces no new ideas.** Re-reading the content from scratch does not reveal spatial opportunities you have not already explored.
3. **The "infinite time" answer is invisible.** Every proposed change is below the perceptual threshold -- invisible-grain adjustments.

### Wave 6 Cognitive Conditions

4. **The Taste Audit produces genuine satisfaction with SPECIFIC reasons.** Not vague approval but articulated judgment: the builder can name what works and why, grounded in 70+ passes of engagement rather than in external reference.

5. **The pride question produces "yes" without qualification.** The builder looks at the page and feels earned satisfaction -- satisfaction that comes from deep engagement, not from effort invested or passes completed.

6. **The spatial argument survives at ALL viewports (not just desktop).** At 768px, the content's movement is still enacted in spatial form. Responsive behavior preserves content-form coupling rather than defaulting to convention.

7. **The deep re-evaluation has revealed understanding that was not accessible at pass 10.** The builder can name specific things that building taught about the content -- discoveries that emerged from 70+ passes of interpretive building, not from reading alone.

8. **The "infinite time" answer is invisible-grain adjustments (approaching satisfaction).** When the builder imagines infinite time and resources, the only remaining moves are below the perceptual threshold. No spatial ideas remain unbuilt. The content and the page have converged.

9. **The builder's evaluation has transcended research citations into POST-research taste.** Spatial decisions are justified by internalized judgment, not by reference to specific research findings. The research built the taste, but the taste now operates independently.

### The Exhaustion Model

The exhaustion conditions form a composite signal: taste audit produces satisfaction + pride question = yes with specific reasons + infinite time = invisible-grain + deep re-evaluation reveals new understanding + responsive argument survives + evaluation is post-research. All conditions must be true simultaneously. Any single condition failing means the wave is not exhausted.

### Minimum Pass Count

**Minimum passes:** 15. This is anti-compression insurance, not a target. If you hit 15 passes and the exhaustion conditions are not met, continue. The pass count prevents the builder from declaring satisfaction at pass 8 due to fatigue or habituation. But 15 passes of shallow engagement do not satisfy the gate -- the cognitive conditions are the real test.

**Exhaustion signal:** The taste audit produces genuine satisfaction. The pride question produces "yes" without qualification. The "infinite time" answer is invisible-grain.
